YOUR AGENT + LAUNCHBASE

From this demo
to your own app.

Same starting point. Completely your idea. The source is free to use and change.

  1. Download your preferred version

    Both have the same design and features. Choose the language you want to work with.

    Next.js source ↓Python source ↓

  2. Open it in your coding agent

    Extract the folder, open it in Claude Code, Cursor or your preferred coding agent, then paste this:

    Read AGENTS.md and https://launchbase.build/agent.md. Connect to my Launchbase account or help me create one, then configure and deploy this template as a new app using the included schema and runtime setup instructions. Verify login, private requests and the test email, give me the live URL, and ask what I want to change.

  3. Approve the connection. Make it yours.

    Open the approval link your agent gives you. Tell it your app’s name, audience and what you want to change. It handles the setup and deployment.

    The source is free. Launchbase hosting starts with its available trial; ongoing hosting follows the current plans. Your agent must explain costs before paid actions.
